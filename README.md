# The AI Timeline — by PUJIVERSE

An interactive history of artificial intelligence, from Jacquard's punched-card loom (1804) and Ada Lovelace's first published program (1843) to the models released in October 2026.

**A PUJIVERSE project.** Data as of October 28, 2026. Live at https://aitimeline.pujiverse.com

## What's inside

- **Timeline** — 1804 → today on a glowing center line, in nine eras: 207 events, each with its own story page and a link to the matching chapter of the History of AI video series (or "coming soon" until that part is out).
- **Videos** — History of AI on the [Pujiverse AI](https://www.youtube.com/@pujiverseai) YouTube channel, in six parts. Part 1 (1843–1973) is out; Parts 2–6 (1974–today) are coming soon. To release a part, fill in its `id` in `VIDEOS` — thumbnails, chapter links and the era video card switch on automatically.
- **Release Tracker** — releases from 2022 to today, in list or calendar view, filterable by company, type and open vs. closed, with same-day clashes highlighted and expected releases shown in their month.
- **Coming Soon** — announced, reported, rumored and cancelled releases, each with its source.
- **Model Library** — a *Current versions* view for 16 products with expandable version histories, plus 100+ model pages with lineage, change logs, specs and side-by-side comparison.
- **AI Tools & Websites**, **Pioneers** (33 people, with the stories each one appears in, including "hidden pioneers"), **AI Winters**, **How AI Is Generated** (with a tokenizer demo), **Companies** and **Models Today**.
- **AI Guide** — a built-in chatbot that is always on: it answers with Claude when the page is opened on claude.ai, and offline from the site's own data everywhere else. It can also tell you which video and chapter covers an event.

## Run it

It's one self-contained file. Open `index.html` in a browser, or host it with GitHub Pages.
